Write the Learning Process, Not Only the Model Conclusion

A useful learning note is not a tutorial paraphrase. It keeps the question, the experiment path, and the boundary of the conclusion together.

I used to wait until I fully understood something before making notes. The result was that most of it stayed in browser tabs and unreliable memory: neither digested nor reusable.

Now I try to record three things: where I began, where I got stuck, and what changed in my judgement.

The smallest useful structure for a learning note

  1. Question: What did I not know before?
  2. Path: Which sources did I use, and what did I try?
  3. Conclusion: What can I explain now, and what can I still not explain?
  4. Next step: Which hypothesis should be tested next?

This does not require pretending to know everything. It simply gives a future version of me a faster way back into the context.
