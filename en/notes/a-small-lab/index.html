Leave a Table for an Unfinished Agent Idea

An engineering experiment is not a product plan. It records hypotheses, prototypes, and explorations that do not yet have an answer.

阅读中文版 →

Not every idea deserves a project, but many deserve a thoughtful first try.

The “lab” is for that kind of work: a weekend prototype, an automation script, or a workflow whose value is still uncertain. It may fail or be left aside. What matters is that the path remains visible.

The next experiment can begin with one sentence: what am I trying to test?
