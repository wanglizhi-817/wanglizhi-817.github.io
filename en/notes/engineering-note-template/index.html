What a Recommender-Model Experiment Should Leave Behind

More useful than remembering that a metric improved is keeping how the problem was defined, how the experiment was designed, and where the conclusion stops.

Engineering lessons expire quickly; a useful retrospective does not. After a debugging session, leave at least one record that another person can understand.

A retrospective template

Observation and impact

Start with what actually changed for users and the system, rather than immediately pasting logs.

Investigation path

Which hypotheses were disproved? Which metrics genuinely narrowed the search space? Those often transfer better than the final patch.

Root cause and repair

Separate trigger, root cause, short-term mitigation, and durable fix. “Optimize it” is not a concrete action.

Preventing recurrence

Add monitoring, tests, documentation, or design constraints so the same kind of issue becomes harder to repeat.
